# Week 3 (W23): Jun 01 – Jun 07, 2026 — The Diary Is Born, the Field Learns to Breathe

Field Lab gets stress-tested into stability while a whole second app — the diary — is designed, built, and redesigned six times before the week is out.

## Narrative

This is the week the codebase stops being a design experiment and starts being a product with organs. 672 observations, 129 sessions, and — remarkably for a week this size — almost no wasted motion: nearly everything that gets built this week is still standing at the end of it.

**Capture bar, four times over.** The week opens mid-thought, picking up the Present-zone accessibility polish from the prior sprint (WCAG AA verified on the dimmest constellation chips by 12:53p) and immediately pivoting into a full capture-bar rethink. Federica's brief is process-driven: "rethink from scratch." Claude runs a contrast audit and finds the clay accent (#22D3EE) fails in light mode, ships an instrument-panel command-line redesign, then within the same afternoon rebuilds it again as an inline quick-idea text input with a hidden-caret UX (S515), and reclassifies it a third time as a "quick IDEA line" with type separation (S516). The capture bar becomes the site of a broader color migration too — accent.clay is split off from `type.todo` to kill a collision, ON_CLAY is retired in favor of scheme-aware `onClay`, and a light-mode failure on amber forces a fixed `ON_AMBER` constant (3082–3088). This on-accent-text pattern (`ON_DANGER`, `ON_SIGNAL`, `ON_AMBER`) recurs all week as new saturated surfaces get added — a quiet signature of how Field Lab enforces contrast: hardcode the ink per background rather than derive it.

**The home screen gets torn down and rebuilt in named parts.** Stakes goes through two full "propose 3 alternatives" rethink cycles in one afternoon (S522, S523) — first a fuel-gauge burndown, rejected same-session, then a uniform heat-tiered list (Direction B), which itself gets rethought four days later (S574–S576) into an agenda-line format with a "Done this week" section, strikethrough completion, and a Clear-with-confirmation affordance. `RunwayGauge` is built, then declared orphaned, then deleted — the week's first fully closed-loop build/replace/delete arc. `StakeRow` is extracted as its own molecule, then gets sketch-icon glyphs (hand-drawn SVG, via a new `react-native-svg` dependency) replacing the checkmark and color dots. The detail screen goes through an equally aggressive rethink (S526–S529): Alternative A ("Signal Rail") is chosen, `DetailActionBar` is refactored from an array of actions to a single primary action, and the screen is fixed to derive its type and color from the database entry rather than an unreliable URL param — a real bug (3409, 3412) with a clean fix.

**The "idea" type resurfaces from a data-model wound.** Mid-afternoon Claude discovers that a prior migration silently consolidated "idea" into "someday" at the schema level while the UI still assumed idea was a live, distinct type — voice capture was quietly broken (3171: "Voice capture feature is broken by missing schema support"). This is the week's one real production bug, and it's diagnosed and fixed in under an hour: schema updated, Migration 6 written to repair existing databases, and idea restored end-to-end (3178–3179). It's also the seed for the diary feature two days later.

**Diary: from zero to a shipped tab in one overnight session, then rebuilt five more times.** Late on Jun 4 (session S639 onward, running past 10:30pm), Federica pivots from clarifying home-screen scope into designing the diary as a genuinely separate surface — deliberately isolated from the actionable board, exactly as CLAUDE.md later documents it. In about ninety minutes: a `diary_entries` table (migration 7), a `useDiary` hook, mood chips, a bottom-sheet mood selector, voice recording, and a fifth tab slot (Field / Incoming / Add / Diary / Calendar) all land. Then Federica reverses the organizing principle entirely — mood tagging is scrapped in favor of idea-linking (S647), with migration 8 adding `linked_entry_id`, a `LinkSheet` with search, note-count badges surfacing on home idea chips, and a `RelatedNotes` component reflecting diary content back onto the detail screen. The diary filter bar alone gets redesigned five times in under ten minutes of session timestamps (S655–S659), chasing the same goal — stop the mood chips from eating vertical space — via underline labels, then icon-triggered sheets, then split row layouts. This is Field Lab's UI-iteration pattern distilled: many small, fully-committed passes rather than one considered design.

**Two late structural moves close the week.** First, a full font migration: Inter is replaced project-wide by Host Grotesk (21 hardcoded references swept in one pass), landing minutes before the week's timestamps jump from Jun 5 to Jun 7 in the source data — implying a multi-day gap the log doesn't narrate. Second, on Jun 7, navigation is restructured: Incoming and Calendar move out of the tab bar into the app header behind a notification badge and a kebab menu, and both screens migrate to a shared `Stack.Screen`-level header pattern (`ScreenHeader`, replacing `ListScreenHeader`), closing out a small object-oriented cleanup with a branded `CalendarIcon`. The week's final act is the empty-state rethink for the home field — Claude proposes multiple strategies (`ConstellationConsole`, `OrbitConsole`) and ships an animated "orbit console": five entry-type dots that breathe, scale by count, glow by urgency, and — after two follow-on bug fixes for dark-mode ring visibility and animation invisibility at max dot size — respond to tap with a spoken "focus line"読み instead of an immediate capture modal. It's the most animation-heavy work of the project to date and it's still being iterated on in the week's last logged session.

## Threads continued
- Present-zone accessibility polish (from Week 2) — closed out at the top of the week with verified WCAG AA contrast.
- Field Lab as the locked design system — held all week; no rebrand reversal. Widget and Watch targets are migrated onto Field Lab tokens (`DesignSystem.swift`), extending it past the phone app for the first time.
- Home-polish backlog — substantially executed: capture bar, stakes, detail screen, greeting, and empty state all shipped, closing most items logged in Week 2's backlog entry.

## Threads opened
- The diary as a second, deliberately non-actionable surface — new data model, new tab, five rapid redesigns of its filter/composer UI, ending the week mid-iteration (composer being moved to a bottom dock with focus-based collapse).
- Idea-linking as a cross-cutting concept — diary notes, home constellation chips, and the detail screen all now reference the same "linked idea" relationship.
- Orbit console — an animated, tap-to-read empty/populated state for the home field, the most technically ambitious single component of the project so far (Reanimated breathing, dynamic geometry, spoken focus lines).
- Android speech recognizer — added in parallel to the iOS module, extending voice capture cross-platform.
- Navigation restructure — tabs reduced from a five-tab layout back down by moving Incoming/Calendar into the header; a `Stack.Screen`-header convention now exists for non-tab routes.

## Threads resolved
- "idea" entry type — schema/UI mismatch fully repaired (Migration 6); voice capture restored.
- RunwayGauge — deprecated and deleted after Stakes redesign made it redundant; the codebase stayed lint-clean throughout.
- Present-zone kicker-color contrast (open item from Week 2 carry-forward) — confirmed fixed this week.
- Font system — Inter replaced by Host Grotesk everywhere, including theme.ts documentation comments.

The orbit console's tap-to-read focus-line feature is freshly shipped and unverified against real device testing (simulators were reported unavailable earlier in the week). And the Week 1 P0 calendar accessibility violations still haven't been mentioned since Week 1 — either fixed off-log or still silently open.
