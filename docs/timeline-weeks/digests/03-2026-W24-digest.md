# Week 4 (W24): Jun 08 – Jun 14, 2026 — Five Types Become Three

A week that opens on diary-composer housekeeping and detonates into a full entity-model amputation: event and someday are cut from the schema's conceptual surface, and the app rewrites its own reason for existing.

## Narrative

The week starts small. Session 731 polishes DiaryComposer — a clear button, a send flow that opens the link sheet instead of firing immediately (#4235–4241) — the kind of ergonomic fussing that closed out Week 3. Session 740 knocks out three backlog items in one pass: custom confirm-alerts with a "don't ask again" preference, inline editing on the detail screen, and list filtering with hierarchy (#4299–4312). The ConfirmSheet/useConfirm refactor and the inline-edit lifecycle are clean, unremarkable wins — Claude finds and fixes a real bug along the way (`draftFromEntry` wasn't parsing `recurrence_rule` out of its stored JSON string, #4307–4309).

Then, late on Jun 9, session 743 asks a much bigger question: should Projects become a top-level organizing entity? Session 744 answers by declaring all five phases of *that* pivot complete by 11:32pm the same night (#4313–4316) — schema v9, migration 9, a new `taxonomy.ts` semantic layer, project CRUD wired end-to-end through `DatabaseContext`, `useDatabase`, and two new screens (Projects List, Project Detail). It's the fastest full-stack feature build logged so far: conception to typed, routed, migrated reality in under two hours.

Jun 10 opens with an experiment in throwing things away: session 752 asks Claude to rebuild `index.tsx` from scratch without reusing existing organisms, "to test model ownership." Two builds get produced and both get replaced same-day — "The Wire" live-telemetry interface (#4386), then an off-clock deck with post-it cards and two-tap focus (#4430), which itself gets redesigned mid-session into scroll-driven single-tap navigation (S754). None of it survives past Jun 11.

Because Jun 11 is when the real story starts. Session 756 asks Claude to lock the entity model: events removed, someday demoted to a render-time badge, palette cut from five colors to three. What follows (#4445–4582, roughly 4:00pm–5:04pm) is the single largest sustained architectural operation of the project's log — over 130 observations in one hour, systematically hunting `event`/`someday` references through every layer: theme tokens, diary moods, the capture-and-file modal, calendar (converted read-only, FAB removed), the app menu, list.tsx (five separate stale-reference sweeps), SketchIcon glyphs, focus-line roll-call, dev-seed data, and — notably — the iOS widget's own `DesignSystem.swift`, which gets its dead `typeEvent`/`typeSomeday` color definitions stripped in step with the phone app. Twenty orphan components get deleted outright (#4545) once nothing references the old types anymore. This is the ADHD-second-brain repositioning promised by BACKLOG.md finally executed as code, not just prose — PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md get rewritten same-day to match (#4450–4458).

The week closes on Jun 14 (a three-day gap in the log) with capture-resolver getting an aerodynamic redesign — animated edge-bar color feedback, a "FILE AS" kicker, fixed-dark-in-both-schemes theming validated against WCAG contrast twice (#4656, #4658) — and a fresh wave of molecule extraction (ResolverVerb, ResolverValue, ResolverSep, PenLine, ValueRow, WhenStepper, ExactWhen). Session 800 opens work on DirectOverview as a proper organism with swipeable done/delete rows, picking up a thread this digest's carry-forward flagged as unresolved last week.

## Threads

**Continued:** Field Lab remains unquestioned — the fixed-dark capture-resolver bar and its WCAG verification are refinements within the system, not challenges to it. Diary composer's send/link flow reached a resting state early in the week.

**Opened:** The Projects entity (schema v9, full CRUD, two screens) — built and then almost immediately subordinated to the bigger three-type pivot. Deadline horizons (Day/Week/Month/Year closing windows). The database-clear bug (projects surviving a "Clear database" action) — reported three separate times (S759, S761, #4599) without a confirmed fix landing in this log.

**Resolved:** Events and someday-as-a-type are gone from the codebase, UI, widget, and documentation — one of the cleanest, most thorough single-session refactors in the project's history. The three off-clock-deck/Wire homepage rebuilds from Jun 10 were explicitly experimental and explicitly discarded.

## Cliffhanger

Session 778, the week's last real checkpoint, is a status check on the pivot branch: mutual-exclusivity fix for the capture resolver is staged but uncommitted, and the database-clearing bug's resolution is unclear. The log then jumps three days to Jun 14, where nobody re-checks the database bug — DirectOverview and capture-resolver polish take over instead. Does the project bug ever get fixed?
