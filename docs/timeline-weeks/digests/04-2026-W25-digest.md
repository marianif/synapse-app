# Week 5 (W25): Jun 17 – Jun 21, 2026 — DirectOverview Ships, Atlas Canvas Gets a Reason to Exist

A quiet 63-observation week: one carried-over feature lands clean, a new component sits unused for three days, then gets rescued with a voice.

## Narrative

The week opens by finishing what Week 4 started. DirectOverview — the toggle-filter, swipeable-row organism begun in S800 — gets its last mile in S801: DirectRow drops its heat-encoded edge-bar for a minimal type-dot (EntryDot), a stray "kicker" TextType error gets caught and fixed within the same minute it's introduced, and the whole thing gets extracted into molecules (`DirectFilterBar`, `DirectRow`) plus a new `lib/direct-when.ts` utility module. DirectOverview itself shrinks to "pure orchestration layer" — the pattern this codebase keeps reaching for: build it fat, then extract until the top-level component just wires things together. No drama, no discarded rebuilds this time — just the previous week's unfinished thread getting tied off in under an hour.

Then a genuine new complaint surfaces: S809 names "the navigation dead-end to @app/projects.tsx" — projects existed but had no home-screen path in or out. The fix is a list/grid layout toggle, persisted via `useUiPreference`, followed next session (S810) by grid cards that show actual entity content instead of bare counts.

The week's real character introduction is Atlas Canvas. It's flagged twice in one evening (S819, then again 4962-4963 the next session) as mounted but functionally orphaned — sitting on the projects page with no integration into the app's workflow, no relationship to FieldGreeting on the home page. S820 and S821 fix that in a single dense evening: a position-storage layer with `movedAt` timestamps, an "Atlas voice utterance library with deterministic priority ladder," an observations engine, and a new `AtlasMarginLine` molecule threading a line of Atlas commentary into FieldGreeting itself. By S822, the ambition escalates again — a full redesign from "cartesian spatial visualization" to a pinning-based project focus selector, with an explicit instruction to validate the Pin Board spec before building.

Small housekeeping threads close out the week: predefined project ordering removed in favor of drag-and-drop (Jun 20), an orphaned `section-layout-menu` component deleted a day after being made customizable (Jun 20→21) — built, then cut, inside 24 hours.
