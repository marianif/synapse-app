# Week 2 (W22): May 30 – May 31, 2026 — Three Rebrands in Two Days

The design system got named, migrated, and renamed twice before the paint dried.

## Narrative

The week opens quietly: CLAUDE.md gets a documentation pass (package manager, notifications layer, watch-connectivity module), committed and merged to main by 10:54pm on May 30. Routine maintenance — the kind of beat established the prior week. Then, three minutes later, everything changes: S460 kicks off a "flow rebrand," and the house that Week 1 mapped and named "The Kinetic Equilibrium" gets torn down before its ink is dry.

A forensics scan (2563-2564) finds "significant visual incoherence" — color, spacing, and typography drift across the app. Federica's brief for the new direction is explicit and personal: a warm, ADHD-optimized, capture-first interface, true light/dark parity, replacing the "corporate" Kinetic Equilibrium system entirely. Mobbin MCP gets pulled in for design reference research (to-do/notes app patterns, input flows, home layouts) — a new tool in the kit. By 11:54pm, direction #1 is locked: **"The Field"** — warm oat-cream/soot-brown, Fraunces serif + Inter sans, pastel palette, soft radii. `brand.md` is written. Ambitious scope is declared for the migration: 45 files, 223 color tokens, 190 violations, 3 phases.

May 31 is the marathon. The migration tooling itself becomes a character: a `migration-scan.mjs` orchestration bug undercounts the true violation surface (44 reported vs. 190 actual) because scope resolution only walks screens, not the 39 component files that also import theme tokens — diagnosed and hand-corrected mid-afternoon (2698-2716). Phases 1 through 3 land through the afternoon: token file deployed, `useTheme()` hook introduced, deprecated `EntryAccent`/`Surface`/`Brand` shim tokens hunted down and replaced file-by-file (day-cell, week-strip, fab, custom-tab-bar, error-boundary). A `fab.tsx` JSX-corruption bug (stripped braces from a token reference, 2761) and a failed scripted-import pass (Phase 2 manual edits reverted to HEAD, 2779-2780) show migration-by-script hitting real limits — Claude rewrites `migrate-phase-2.mjs` to be JSX-aware and hits 100% deterministic migration on the retry (2784).

By 3:03pm Phase 1 of color migration is documented complete, PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md rewritten for The Field. But the theming *mechanism* itself — a dark-locked shim, zero files on `useTheme()` — still needed replacing. S487-S488 drive that: AsyncStorage-backed theme persistence, ThemeContext, root layout rewiring, and by 4:04-4:05pm the entire compat shim block is deleted from `constants/theme.ts`, DESIGN.md and PRODUCT.md updated to reflect light/dark as "live and complete."

Then the home screen gets rebuilt on top of it (S489-S503): FieldGreeting → FieldTile bento tiles → urgency-weighted hierarchy → mock data seeding → and at 4:38pm, **direction #2**: Federica rejects The Field as insufficiently "activating" despite "activation" being the core brief keyword. A cooler, instrument-panel-style direction is explored and locked by 5:29pm as **"Field Lab"** — the name that survives into CLAUDE.md's present-day description of the app. Home screen is rebuilt again as a two-zone board (Stakes/Present), with a talking `FieldBriefing` molecule, a `StakesRunway` fuel-gauge visualization for deadlines, and four experimental Present-zone layouts (Constellation, Columns, Stack, Mosaic) A/B'd live on-device before settling on a Cloud+Mosaic split. The week ends mid-polish, fixing contrast in "aged constellation chips."
