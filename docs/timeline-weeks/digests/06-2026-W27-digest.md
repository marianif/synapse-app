# Week 7 (W27): Jun 30 – Jul 02, 2026 — Everything At Once

The trough breaks: one accessibility audit, one hero-gauge deletion, and then a single Jul 2 evening detonates into a dozen sessions rebuilding half the app.

## Narrative

Week 7 opens quietly enough. Jun 30 is a Flow audit day — `app/project.tsx` and its sheet collaborators get scanned for accessibility, and the results aren't flattering: 27 violations in project.tsx's collaborators, then 28 more across six sheet molecules. The pattern is consistent and almost funny in its consistency — inner row components are properly labeled, but every sheet's outer frame, every overlay Pressable, is accessibility-invisible. It's the same "container forgets what its children remember" bug, six times over. Hardcoded magic numbers turn up too, violating the token system DESIGN.md insists on. None of it gets fixed this week — it's audit only, filed and left. The one concrete action from the day is small: the open-count gauge in the project HERO section, built up with background and padding just hours earlier, gets torn back out at 11:54 PM as redundant, along with its now-dead `openCount` useMemo calculation. Built and cut in the same session — the churn pattern from prior weeks, compressed into single-digit hours.

Then Jul 1 is nearly silent — just a dev-server start — and Jul 2 happens.

The day begins with real product work: `app/projects.tsx` gets a search bar, and project creation migrates from an inline shelf form to a FAB-triggered composer modal, mirroring the capture pattern used elsewhere in the app. Keyboard-avoidance bugs get chased and fixed with animated tracking tied to safe-area insets. Then, in a genuinely good bit of cleanup, the bespoke `CreateRow` composer gets replaced wholesale by reusing `ManualBar` — the same component `app/(tabs)/index.tsx` already uses — collapsing two divergent creation flows into one.

But the day's real story is the empty-state reckoning. A brainstorm about "no projects" UX turns into implementation: **default projects now seed on first launch** — a macro-life-areas fixture (People among them, its emoji upgraded from 🤝 to the warmer 👥) wired through new schema-versioning infrastructure, with `clearAllData` now correctly resetting the seeding flag so a cleared database re-seeds instead of staying empty. This directly answers Week 5–6's long-dangling "database-clear bug" and the Jun 23 "workbench environment" brief gets superseded rather than resumed — the projects screen goes a different direction entirely.

From there Federica runs a marathon of Flow reviews back-to-back: `app/(tabs)/index.tsx` gets picked apart (symbol collisions between duplicate `signalFor`/`greetingFor`, a dead unactivated "focus" feature in `FieldGreeting`, an O(n²) rollup in `ProjectsOverview` fixed to O(n), a page-clamping bug in `DirectOverview`, a stale-Date bug in the calendar hook) — real bugfixes, not just prose. `app/detail.tsx` gets redesigned four times in one evening (tonal pressed-states, radius tightening, then a full consolidation of four scattered telemetry molecules into one new `DetailHero`, with the orphaned originals deleted outright). `DirectOverview` gains empty-state messaging with type-specific color accents and a capture CTA, then gets extended so ideas surface as first-class direct rows alongside todos and deadlines — a genuine Equal Volume principle correction, with PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md both updated to match. The evening closes with click-outside-to-dismiss and entrance/exit animations on the capture bars, then a claude-mem corpus built over the day's own work — the log documenting itself in real time.

## Where We Are

As of Jul 2, 2026, the codebase just had its loudest day in the log — 181 observations this week against single digits the week before. Several real threads closed: the database-clear bug is fixed, DirectOverview's dead space now has purpose, detail.tsx's telemetry sprawl is consolidated into one molecule, and default project seeding means new users never hit a truly empty board.

But volume isn't resolution everywhere. The two freshly-logged accessibility audits (55 violations combined, project.tsx and six sheet molecules) are filed, not fixed — the same overlay-labeling gap the log has flagged before, now larger. Atlas Canvas's pinning-redesign pivot, gated on a "Pin Board specification" at the end of Week 5, has not been mentioned in three weeks running; it's the log's oldest open question and this week didn't touch it. The capture resolver mutual-exclusivity fix, last "staged but uncommitted" in Week 4, is still unmentioned. Diary composer and OrbitConsole tap-to-read remain cold.

Federica's register this week swung from single terse directives to a rapid-fire sequence of Flow-review prompts, several explicitly iterating on the same file (detail.tsx redesigned across four consecutive sessions until it stopped looking "sloppy"). That iterate-until-right pattern, plus a same-day corpus-building pass over the evening's own commits, suggests a workflow that's begun consciously archiving itself as it goes — the log noticing the log. Whatever comes next inherits a genuinely healthier home screen and projects flow, a larger accessibility backlog, and Atlas Canvas still waiting in the wings.
