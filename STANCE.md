# Stance

**Feel** · what this should feel like to use
> A dense, live panel of information, annotated in your own handwriting. Precise data, warm voice.

**Anti-feel** · what it should never feel like
> Twee, not warm. The handwritten layer stays personal and warm; it never turns cute, mascot-y, or decorative.

**Reference (love)** · one product we'd be proud to be mistaken for
> A field notebook or lab log, because the entries are dense and precise but kept by hand, never sterile.

**Reference (refuse)** · one product we'd be embarrassed to be mistaken for
> A pastel habit-tracker with streaks and badges, Habitica-style gamification.

**Forbidden word** · one adjective a reviewer must never use
> Basic. The fear of collapsing into just another todo or productivity app.

**Trade** · easier to use vs. more distinct
> Easier to use, but never at the cost of the identity. Comprehension wins close calls, but the field-notebook-panel character can't be sanded away to get there.
