## Position

**The Field — the New Yorker, if it organized your life.**

The Field is a bento board of your whole brain. Open it and everything you are
carrying is already there: bills, ideas, to-dos, events, somedays — each in its
own soft-tinted tile, color-coded and packed with the real, tappable items
rather than a count to click through. A warm Fraunces serif headline greets you
like a person, not a dashboard. Capture lives in an always-on clay bar pinned to
the bottom, one tap from any screen.

The system is built for an ADHD glance: out of sight is out of mind, so nothing
is hidden behind a "today" filter, and nothing on screen is a dead label. It is
bold the way an editorial page is bold — through confident type and a
disciplined pastel system that survives both light and dark — never through
gradients, badges, or decoration.

- **Scene:** A capture-first person opens the app mid-thought to shoot a bill, an idea, or a to-do down before it's gone, and needs their entire mental field present and actionable at a glance.
- **Is:** activating · editorial · warm
- **Is not:** corporate · gamified · minimalist-empty
- **First 3 seconds:** Everything I'm carrying is right here, and I can act on any of it without hunting.
- **Would be wrong to feel:** Curated-down, filtered, or like another cheerful productivity app with streaks.
- **App type:** tool (capture-first, warm human voice)

## Ruled Out

- No FAB — capture is an always-on bottom bar
- No gradients anywhere (no LinearGradient buttons or glows)
- No pure white `#FFFFFF` / pure black `#000000` — warm extremes only
- No 1px structural borders — tonal layering + color edge-bars only
- No streaks, badges, confetti, or gamification
- No dead count-only tiles — every tile shows real, tappable items
- No curating home down to "today" — everything stays present
- No pastel-illustration mascots or decorative spot art
- The kicker is the only all-caps text — no all-caps body or buttons

## Tokens

### Color — soft tint body, saturated kicker + edge-bar

Each entry type gets a **soft tile-tint** (calm body) plus a **saturated
color** for its kicker label and left edge-bar. Both light and dark tints are
defined per type so pastels survive both schemes.

**Surfaces**
| Token | Light | Dark | Role |
|-------|-------|------|------|
| paper | `#F4EFE6` | `#16140F` | Root background (warm, never #fff/#000) |
| surface | `#FBF7F0` | `#211E18` | Tile body |
| surfaceSubtle | `#EFE8DB` | `#1C1A14` | Recessed / gutter |

**Text**
| Token | Light | Dark | Role |
|-------|-------|------|------|
| ink | `#2A2622` | `#F0EAE0` | Primary |
| inkMuted | `#6B6358` | `#A39B8C` | Metadata / kicker meta |

**Entry types** (tint = body, color = kicker + edge-bar)

### Type — Fraunces + Inter

Warm Fraunces serif for the greeting headline, tile/section headers, and the
human voice; Inter for dense tile content and UI. Hierarchy = **serif vs sans + size**.

| Step | Size / LH | Weight | Font | Use |
|------|-----------|--------|------|-----|
| display | 30 / 36 | 600 | Fraunces | Home greeting headline |
| title | 22 / 28 | 600 | Fraunces | Tile / section headers |
| item | 17 / 23 | 500 | Inter | Entry titles in tiles |
| body | 14 / 20 | 400 | Inter | Entry detail / supporting |
| kicker | 11 / 14 | 600 | Inter | All-caps type label (+0.6 tracking) |

### Spacing — 4pt base
Rhythm `4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32`. Keeps the existing 4pt base and fixes
the off-beat `3px` / `14px` values forensics flagged. Tighter inside tiles,
generous between sections.

### Radius — large/soft
`sm 10 · md 14 · lg 18 · pill 999`. Tiles feel like cards you could pick up.
Replaces the current sharp 4/6px corners.

### Motion — expressive but disciplined
Durations `fast 160 · base 240 · slow 360`. Easing
`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`. Tiles spring in subtly on open
(`withSpring`, damping ~16) for ProMotion life; everything respects
`useReducedMotion`. Transform/opacity only.
**Banned:** gradient-glow pulses, bounce/elastic on nav, looping motion, confetti.

### Elevation — layered, warm-tinted
Flat paper → tiles with a soft warm lift (`rgba(80,50,30,0.10)`, y8, blur20 /
android elevation 2) → capture bar (`rgba(80,50,30,0.16)`, y-2, blur16 /
elevation 8). No pure-black shadows, no borders.

## Rules

- **Dark mode:** first-class light **and** dark, full parity. Each type has a
  light and a dark tint of the same hue; saturated kicker/edge-bar colors are
  shared. Resolve via one `useTheme()` hook at the provider level — no inline
  `colorScheme` ternaries in components.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG AA. Verify ink-on-tint and saturated-kicker-on-tint
  pairs clear 4.5:1 in both schemes. Dynamic Type to 2x, no fixed-height tiles.
  Touch targets 44pt iOS / 48dp Android (capture bar + tile items included).
